#### 3\. Staying in Control

FairPlay encourages the Club members to actively participate in the management of their gambling account and thus offers a whole range of responsible gambling features:

#### 3.1 Set Deposit Limits

Take financial control of your gambling account with the ability to set your preferred deposit limits.

Deposit limits will apply across all accounts you hold with the respective brands offered by FairPlay Limited (Hippodrome, Spin Casino, Bingo on the Box and Dream Bingo).

Deposit limits may be set for the following periods:

- Daily
- Weekly (Monday to Sunday)
- Monthly (calendar month)

At your request, deposit limits are subject to being reduced, increased or removed altogether. An increase or complete removal of deposit limits will take effect after a 24 hour buffer, whilst a decrease of deposit limits will be effective immediately.

If you would like to set or adjust a deposit limit, please login to your account and go to “Set Limits”.

Alternatively, you can contact Client Services for assistance with the same.

#### 3.2 Take a Break

At FairPlay we understand that a Club member’s personal/ financial circumstances are subject to change and that there will be times when it is only right to take a break from gambling. We offer the “Take A Break” in the following periods:

- 24 hours
- 48 hours
- 1 week
- 2 weeks
- 3 weeks
- 4 weeks
- 6 weeks

Upon receiving your request, Team FairPlay will temporarily suspend all accounts you hold with brands managed by FairPlay Group until your break period has been fulfilled.

**NOTE:** Once the “Take A Break” period expires, your account will automatically be re-activated unless an extension is requested and you will once again have access to all the platforms provided by FairPlay Group.

#### 3.3 Activity Statement

Information regarding every single of your gambling activities on FairPlay can be viewed for up to 30 days. The information includes the start date and time of your gambling session as well as its duration.

Further information regarding your activity on FairPlay can also be viewed for a period of 90 days. The information includes details about your account balance, gambling history (including bet amounts, winnings and losses), deposits, withdrawals and other related transactions.

FairPlay allows you to set a “Session Reminder” so you can keep track of the time you spend gambling.

Upon expiry of the time period selected, an automated message reminding you of how long you've been playing will appear on the screen and requires you to select one of the following three options:

- Continue; or
- Exit (end the game); or
- View Account History

**NOTE:** The Session Reminder set within the My Account feature does not apply to Live Casino games. You can set a separate Session Reminder within our Live Casino games offering via the ‘Game Session Settings’ feature under Settings.

If you would like to be notified about how long you've been playing, please login in and then set a Session Reminder. Alternatively, you can contact Client Services for personal assistance.

#### 3.5 Account Closure

If you feel like taking some time away from gambling, or it just doesn’t seem entertaining anymore, you can simply close your account. You can re-open the account at any time by contacting Client Services. If you would prefer to just take some time off instead, please refer to our “Take a Break” option, which will restrict access to your account from 24 hours up to 6 weeks.
