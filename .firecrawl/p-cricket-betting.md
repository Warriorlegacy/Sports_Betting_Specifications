## Types of ESports Bets

ESports betting offers a variety of wager types, catering to both newcomers and seasoned bettors. Here are some of the most common types of ESports bets:

Match Winner: This is the most straightforward bet, where you predict which team or player will win a particular match.

Tournament Winner: Here, bettors predict which team or player will emerge victorious in an entire tournament. Given the unpredictability and length of some tournaments, these bets can offer higher rewards but also come with increased risks.

Handicap Betting: In matches where one team is a clear favourite, bookmakers might offer handicap bets. This means the stronger team must win by a certain margin for bets on them to pay out.

Prop Bets (Proposition Bets): These are wagers on specific events within a match. Examples include betting on the player with the first kill, the number of objectives taken by a team, or the total kills in a game.

Live Betting: Also known as in-play betting, this allows bettors to place wagers while a match is ongoing. Odds can change rapidly based on in-game events, making this a dynamic and exciting betting format.

Over/Under Bets: Bookmakers set a number for a particular statistic (e.g., total kills in a match), and bettors wager whether the actual number will be over or under that set number.
